About

From the yard floor
to the regression table.

Muhammad Fauzani Akbar studies port operations from the ground up — measuring dwelling time, throughput, and yard occupancy at Pelabuhan Belawan — then builds the statistical and machine learning models that explain why those numbers move the way they do.

A recent graduate of the D4 Administrasi Niaga (Business Administration) program at Politeknik Negeri Medan, he combines hands-on port operations experience with the analytical rigor to model it — comfortable both running the terminal and analyzing it.
