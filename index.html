Undergraduate thesis · 2026

Modeling Yard Occupancy at Pelabuhan Belawan

Problem

Pelabuhan Belawan needed a clearer read on what actually drives Yard Occupancy Ratio (YOR) — the KPI that signals when a container yard is running out of room.

Approach

Built a multiple regression model with Dwelling Time Ekspor and Throughput TEUs as predictors of YOR, run in SPSS. The dataset was limited to 15 months of data captured after Autogate 3 went live (n = 15) — deliberately excluding the pre-Autogate period so the model wouldn't mix two different operational regimes into one structural break. Power BI dashboards were built to present the findings at the thesis defense.

Conference paper · ICWET 2026

Predicting Sugarcane Yield with Random Forest

Problem

PT Perkebunan Nusantara I Regional I, Helvetia, North Sumatra, needed a reliable way to forecast sugarcane yield ahead of harvest to plan processing capacity and logistics.

Approach

Chose a Random Forest model over simpler linear approaches because the agronomic relationships involved are non-linear and the field records are noisy — Random Forest handles both without heavy preprocessing or feature scaling. Built a live Streamlit demo of the model for the conference talk.

Strategy diagnostic

Why Belawan's Terminal Round Trip Runs 2.4× KPI

Problem

Pelabuhan Belawan's average Terminal Round Trip Time (TRT GO) was running far above the operational KPI, and the port sat at a weak #244 in the global CPPI ranking. The initial assumption was a single broken process — that turned out to be wrong.

Approach

Applied Rumelt's Kernel framework, which forces a real diagnosis stage before jumping to a fix — useful here, since it surfaced a different root cause than expected: a fragmented data layer (TOS Nusantara, TBS, and PTOS-M running unintegrated) rather than one single process failure.
